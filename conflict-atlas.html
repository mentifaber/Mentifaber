U.S. / Israel – Iran War

Major war · Middle East · Fragile ceasefire

Operation Epic Fury, Feb 28 2026. Khamenei killed in strikes; Iran retaliated against US bases in the UAE, Qatar and Bahrain. Hormuz mined and blocked. An MOU was signed Jun 17 — then an IRGC drone struck a Singapore cargo ship on Jun 26. 1,348+ confirmed killed. Nuclear talks continue in Switzerland.

Russia – Ukraine War

Major war · Europe · Active · year five

The largest conflict in Europe since the Second World War, and the most lethal active war on earth — 80,205 deaths in 2025 alone. Year five is dominated by long-range drone warfare and strikes on energy infrastructure. Roughly 350,000 Russian and 140,000 Ukrainian soldiers killed. No ceasefire.

Sudan Civil War — SAF vs RSF

Major war · Africa · Active · year three

The UN calls this the world's worst humanitarian crisis. ~150,000 civilians killed, 12 million displaced, 33.7 million in need of aid. The UAE backs the RSF; Egypt backs the SAF. Gold mining finances both sides. Mass starvation in Darfur.

Myanmar Civil War

Major war · SE Asia · World's longest

Ongoing since 1948 — the longest running civil war in the world. The 2021 coup reignited it at full scale; 15,420+ killed between mid-2024 and mid-2025. China has thrown its weight decisively behind the junta, which has regained the upper hand after a rebel-ascendant 2024.

DR Congo — M23 / Rwanda

Major war · Central Africa · Active

Rwanda-backed M23 rebels captured Goma, the eastern DRC capital. Rwanda has effectively annexed North and South Kivu through proxies. A ceasefire was brokered in June 2025; fighting continues on the ground regardless. Coltan extraction funds it.

Sahel Insurgency — Mali / Niger / Burkina Faso

Major war · West Africa · Expanding

21,185 deaths in 2025. After coups expelled Western forces, al-Qaeda and ISIS filled the vacuum within months. Russia's Africa Corps operates across the region, with documented executions of ethnic Fulani civilians in Mali. Spreading south toward the coast.

Israel – Lebanon / Hezbollah

Major war · Middle East · Active

Re-escalated Mar 2 2026 when the Khamenei assassination triggered Hezbollah rocket salvos and heavy Israeli airstrikes across Lebanon. A fifth round of US-brokered talks is underway in Washington. Jun 21 was the first attack-free day since March.

Israel – Gaza / Hamas

Significant · Middle East · Fragile ceasefire

Began Oct 7 2023. Gaza's civilian infrastructure is largely destroyed after operations resumed in March 2025. A ceasefire was signed; hundreds have been killed since — the gap between an agreement and the ground. 2.3M+ displaced.

Yemen War — Houthis

Significant · Middle East · Active

Active since 2014. Iran-backed Houthis continued striking Israel and Red Sea shipping throughout the 2026 Iran war. Retaliatory strikes have further degraded an already-collapsed state. The arsenal is partly degraded; they remain a significant regional actor.

Somalia — Al-Shabaab

Significant · East Africa · Active

Al-Shabaab, al-Qaeda's strongest affiliate, controls large areas of south-central Somalia and is expanding north. US AFRICOM ran 8+ airstrikes Jan–Mar 2026. Withdrawal of US security assistance risks major expansion. Somalia tops the Fragile States Index.

India – Pakistan / Operation Sindoor

Significant · South Asia · Tense ceasefire

The worst India–Pakistan confrontation in decades. Pakistan declared open war Feb 27 2026; India's Operation Sindoor struck targets across the Line of Control. A ceasefire holds but is fragile — both states are nuclear-armed and under domestic pressure.

Afghanistan – Pakistan Border War

Significant · South Asia · Active

Pakistan bombed Kabul in retaliation for TTP cross-border attacks; the Taliban government refuses to expel TTP fighters. Ongoing missile and drone strikes across the Durand Line, with Chinese mediation attempted and Kashmir running simultaneously.

Nigeria — Multiple Insurgencies

Significant · West Africa · Active

Four conflicts at once: Boko Haram and ISWAP in the northeast, bandit mass-abductions in the northwest, Fulani–farmer clashes in the Middle Belt, IPOB separatism in the southeast. Nigeria remains in the ACLED global top ten.

Haiti — Gang War

Significant · Caribbean · Active

4,500+ killed by political violence in 2025, nearly double 2024. Gangs control most of Port-au-Prince. The failure of international stabilisation, including the Kenya-led mission, enabled it. ACLED ranking jumped from 11th to 8th globally.

Syria / Iraq — ISIS Resurgence

Significant · Middle East · Escalating

Syria's new leader Ahmed al-Sharaa faces rising sectarian violence and an ISIS resurgence. US forces maintain counter-terror operations in both countries. ISIS is actively exploiting the regional distraction of the Iran war to rebuild capacity and territory.

Iran — Internal Instability

Significant · Middle East · Unstable

Late 2025: nationwide protests met with lethal force, 6,000+ killed by the government. Feb 2026: Khamenei killed in US/Israeli strikes, his son installed as successor. Nuclear and missile infrastructure destroyed. Regime legitimacy severely damaged.

Mexico — Cartel War

Significant · North America · Active

8,616 estimated deaths across 2024–25. The Sinaloa Cartel split triggered intensified inter-cartel warfare; CJNG and Sinaloa factions fight each other and the state with military-grade US weapons. The Trump administration has threatened direct strikes inside Mexico.

Cameroon — Ambazonia War

Significant · Central Africa · Active · ignored

Anglophone separatists have fought for an independent Ambazonia since 2016. HRW documents extrajudicial killings, village burnings and arbitrary detention by the military. Schools in anglophone regions have been shut for years. Near-zero Western coverage.

Ethiopia – Eritrea

Active · Horn of Africa · Pre-war tension

Ethiopia wants Red Sea access as Africa's most populous landlocked nation; Eritrea fears invasion. Both accuse the other of arming militias. A war between them would destabilise the entire Horn, and a distracted international community is largely ignoring it.

South Sudan

Active · East Africa · Volatile

Election delays risk renewed conflict between ethnic factions and political militias. The central government is fragile, with massive spillover pressure from Sudan's civil war and some of the worst food insecurity metrics in the world.

Mozambique — Cabo Delgado

Active · Southern Africa · Intensifying

An ISIS-linked insurgency intensifying through 2026, adjacent to major offshore gas projects. Rwandan and SADC troops are deployed; the insurgency persists. Widespread civilian displacement.

Libya — Managed Fragmentation

Active · North Africa · Frozen conflict

Turkey backs the western government; Russia, Egypt and the UAE back Haftar in the east. Africa's largest oil reserves keep both sides at the table without resolution. A major hub for trafficking and weapons flowing from the Sahel into Europe.

West Bank — Israeli Operations

Active · Middle East · Active

Escalating IDF operations, especially in Jenin, driven by settlement expansion, political repression and armed clashes. A worsening humanitarian crisis that complicates any diplomatic process.

South China Sea — China vs Philippines

Active · East Asia · Escalating

China blocked Scarborough Shoal at the peak of the Iran war. Water-cannon and ship-ramming manoeuvres against Philippine supply vessels are ongoing. The US–Philippines mutual defence treaty makes this one of the most dangerous long-term escalation risks on earth.

Bangladesh

Active · South Asia · Growing

Political and religious violence growing after the ouster of PM Sheikh Hasina in 2024. Elections postponed, governance crisis deepening, Islamist factions gaining in the vacuum. Violence against Hindu minorities documented.

Ecuador — Gang War

Active · South America · Active

50+ armed groups including ~40 gangs. ACLED rank 6 globally. Over 1,000 more killed than in 2024. Repeated states of emergency and ongoing prison massacres have made Ecuador one of the most violent nations in the hemisphere within a few years.

Brazil — Gang Violence

Active · South America · Active

Consistently top-ten on the ACLED Conflict Index. PCC and CV factions, alongside former-police militias, operate with near-impunity. Per-capita gang violence rivals active war zones, across both favelas and rural conflict.

Strait of Hormuz Crisis

Active · Persian Gulf · Contested

Iran closed the Strait on Mar 4 — 20% of global oil and LNG passes through it. Crude tanker traffic dropped 95%, LNG 99% at peak. A US counter-blockade ran Apr 13–May 29. 11,000+ seafarers stranded. Reopened Jun 17; the central channel is still mined.

On the method

Why a list

the premise

Conflict coverage collapses into whichever war is currently legible to a newsroom. Cameroon has been at war since 2016 with near-zero Western coverage. Myanmar has been at war since 1948.

A list does not fix that. It does make the omission visible, which is the smallest useful thing a list can do.

the tiers

Three bands — major war, significant, active — assigned by scale and lethality rather than by how much attention each one receives. The ordering is deliberately not the ordering you would get from a front page.

what a ceasefire means here

Several entries are marked as ceasefires and are still killing people. Gaza has a signed ceasefire and hundreds of deaths since. Iran signed an MOU on Jun 17 and a cargo ship was struck on Jun 26.

The agreement and the ground are different objects. The badges say which one is being described.

the 3D version

This data also drives an interactive globe — markers on a rotating earth, tap for the detail. It is a separate build. This page is the readable one, and reading it takes longer, which is appropriate.
